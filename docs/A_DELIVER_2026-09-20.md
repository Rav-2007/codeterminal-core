Two reasons, and the second is the one that decides it. The weaker reason: both
documents are dated records of work done on the fork at a time when the fork was
where the work was, and `BRANCH_STATUS_2026-09-05.md:53-57` discusses the

destroys the evidence of what was believed at the time — the same principle that
forbids editing the release plan's ledger in place. **A register is corrected; a
report is appended to.**

surrounding frame, will resolve `33620636112` against canonical and get a 404.
The fix, if someone wants it, is a one-line frame note at the top of each of the
two documents rather than seven edits — **(U)**, and what would settle it is a
decision about whether dated reports may carry an added header. Not taken here.
