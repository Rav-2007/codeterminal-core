## 8. What CI confirmed about the timing guard, and what it did not

*(Added 2026-09-12, after `build` `34684019860`.)*

The timing guard is the one gate in this pass that **failed its own first
neuter** — it passed green with its detector blinded. "The job is green" is
therefore weaker evidence for it than for an ordinary test, and the three things
worth knowing are not equally established.

### Confirmed — it ran, and it ran on more than one platform

Two independent pieces of evidence, neither of them "the job was green":

1. **The file compiled and ran on the Windows runner.** The derived
   platform-coverage banner moved from `153 of 178 test files compiled; 25 DID
   NOT RUN` at `a0635bf` to **`154 of 179 test files compiled; 25 DID NOT RUN`**
   at `8340022`. The push added **exactly one** test file. The total rose by one
   and **the compiled column rose by one**, while the did-not-run column did not
   move — so the new file is in the compiled set, on `windows/amd64`, not only
   on the Linux runner where it was written.
2. **The vacuity floors are `t.Fatalf`, not log lines.** A walk that found fewer
   than 200 test files, or recognised fewer than 10 duration comparisons, fails
   the test. A green `go (daemon)` job therefore establishes **≥200 files walked
   and ≥10 comparisons recognised on the runner**, independently of what was
   printed.

### NOT confirmed — the counts, and the self-test, are not observable in CI

**`build.yml` runs `go test -race -count=1 ./...` without `-v`.** The guard
reports its scope with `t.Logf`, which `go test` discards for a passing package.
Grepping the full 7,522-line run log for `timing-bound scan` returns **zero
hits**, and for `TimingGuard` **zero hits**.

So:

| B.4 asked | Status |
|---|---|
| It ran | **Confirmed**, twice over, by the evidence above |
| It inspected a non-zero count across six modules | **Lower bound only.** ≥200 files and ≥10 comparisons, from the floors. The actual figures (25 comparisons, 321 files, 6 modules) are **this machine's**, and CI has never printed its own |
| Its self-test executed | **Inferred, not observed.** `TestTimingGuardCanStillFail` carries no build tag, so `go test ./...` runs it and a failure would be red — but no log line names it |

### The repository already solved this, for a sibling gate

`build.yml` runs `TestPlatformCoverageIsStated` **explicitly with `-v`**, in its
own step, with a comment saying why: `go test` swallows the output without it,
and the output *is* the deliverable. **The timing guard has exactly that
property and does not get that treatment.** That is a one-line asymmetry between
two gates of the same kind.

**Recorded, not fixed.** It is a change to `build.yml`, and this unit of work was
scoped to verifying what was already built. Whoever picks it up should note that
the honest version prints the counts rather than asserting them — the difference
between "the gate is green" and "the gate inspected 25 comparisons across 6
modules **here**" is the whole subject of R1.16.
